This Privacy Policy explains how Simply Supported collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit our website or contact us about our services. 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT When you submit an enquiry or booking request through our website, we may collect: • Your name • Your email address • Your preferred service • Your preferred date and time • The approximate number of hours required • Information you provide about your requirements • Any other information you choose to include in your enquiry We may also receive basic technical information about visitors to our website, such as browser or device information, where this is provided automatically by our website hosting or security services. 3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION We use your information to: • Respond to enquiries and booking requests • Check availability and arrange services • Communicate with you about your requested services • Provide and manage services you have requested • Keep appropriate business records • Handle complaints or queries • Protect our website and business from misuse or fraud • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations 4. HOW WE CONTACT YOU If you contact Simply Supported, we may use the email address you provide to respond to your enquiry or communicate with you about services you have requested. We will not use your personal information for unrelated marketing purposes unless we have a lawful basis to do so and, where required, your consent. 5. SHARING YOUR INFORMATION We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may also disclose information where required by law, regulation, court order or to protect our legal rights. 6. HOW LONG WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected. Where information forms part of our business or financial records, we may need to retain it for longer periods to meet legal, accounting or tax requirements. 7. SECURITY We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against accidental loss, unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. However, no website or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. 8. YOUR RIGHTS Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights including: • The right to ask for access to personal information we hold about you • The right to ask for inaccurate information to be corrected • The right to ask for your information to be deleted where there is no lawful reason for us to continue holding it • The right to ask us to restrict how we use your information in certain circumstances • The right to object to certain uses of your information • The right to data portability in certain circumstances You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you believe your personal information has been handled unlawfully. You can find information about the ICO at: https://ico.org.uk/ 9. COOKIES Our website may use essential technologies required for the website to operate correctly.
