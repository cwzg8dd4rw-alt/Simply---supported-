Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Ready & Sorted respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used and protected when you use this website or contact Ready & Sorted about its services.

1. About Ready & Sorted

Ready & Sorted provides flexible administrative and personal assistant support to individuals and small businesses.

For the purposes of applicable data protection legislation, Ready & Sorted is responsible for the personal information collected and used in connection with its services.

2. Information We May Collect

When you contact Ready & Sorted or submit an enquiry through this website, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The service you are interested in
  • Your preferred date or timing
  • Approximate hours requested
  • Details about the task you need help with
  • Other information you choose to provide

We may also receive basic technical information associated with operating, securing and maintaining the website.

3. How We Use Personal Information

Personal information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Discuss your requirements
  • Check availability
  • Provide quotes
  • Arrange and provide agreed services
  • Communicate about bookings
  • Maintain appropriate business records
  • Deal with complaints or queries
  • Protect the website and business against misuse or fraud
  • Meet legal, regulatory, accounting or tax obligations

4. Email and Communications

If you contact Ready & Sorted by email or through the website, your information may be used to respond to your enquiry and communicate with you about a requested service or booking.

Ready & Sorted does not currently send unrelated marketing emails to customers simply because they have made an enquiry.

If marketing communications are introduced in the future, they will be handled in accordance with applicable data protection and electronic marketing laws.

5. Sharing Personal Information

Ready & Sorted does not sell your personal information.

Personal information may be shared where reasonably necessary to operate the business, provide an agreed service or comply with legal obligations.

This may include appropriate service providers such as:

  • Website hosting providers
  • Email providers
  • Payment providers
  • Technology or security providers
  • Accountants or professional advisers where appropriate

Where an agreed PA or errand task requires dealing with a retailer, delivery provider, appointment provider, venue or similar third party, relevant information may be provided where reasonably necessary to complete the task.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or by a competent authority.

6. Legal Basis for Processing

Depending on the circumstances, Ready & Sorted may process personal information where it is necessary to:

  • Take steps at your request before entering into a service arrangement
  • Perform an agreed contract or provide requested services
  • Comply with a legal obligation
